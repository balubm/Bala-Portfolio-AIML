01 — About

From semiconductor fabs to sound studios

I am an applied data scientist and machine learning practitioner with a background spanning deep learning, statistical modeling, and real-world AI systems. I previously worked at Intel, where I applied data science and Design of Experiments to optimize semiconductor manufacturing, improve yield, and automate large-scale analysis pipelines — turning complex data into practical, measurable outcomes in high-stakes production environments.

Since then, I founded and led Sonic Pro Studios, where I combine machine learning with creative technology: generative audio models, CNN-based audio classifiers, and ML-driven workflow optimizations used in production settings. I enjoy bridging research and execution — taking ideas from experimentation to deployment — with a particular focus on applied AI, generative models, and systems that operate reliably at scale.
