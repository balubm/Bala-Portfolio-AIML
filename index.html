01 — About

ML engineering for audio, end to end

My core work is applied deep learning for audio: singing voice conversion and synthesis, harmony generation, stem separation, ASR and forced alignment — plus the DSP around them: pitch tracking, dereverberation, loudness and mastering, formant processing. I own systems end to end: architecture, PyTorch training pipelines, low-latency GPU inference in production, and the A/B experiments that prove a model change actually improves the product.

Two kinds of rigor back that up. 02 — Projects

Selected work

Shipped at ControllaGenerative Audio

Lyric Swap

Type new lyrics; the song keeps its melody, timing, and voice. The pipeline transcribes and force-aligns the original vocal word by word, maps the new syllables onto the existing melodic and rhythmic structure, then re-renders only the changed words with singing-voice synthesis — leaving the rest of the original performance untouched.

Shipped at ControllaAI Harmony

Choir Studio

Turn a single vocal into a full, arranged choir. Voice conversion recasts the performance into distinct singer timbres, harmony parts are arranged across soprano-to-bass ranges with music-theory-aware voicing, and per-voice pitch and timing humanization plus spatial mixing make the ensemble sound like real singers in a real room.
